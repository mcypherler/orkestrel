# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product

**Orkestrel**

*"Never miss the events you'll love."*

---

# Problem

Families regularly miss concerts, gigs and live events because:

- They hear about them too late.
- Tickets sell out quickly.
- Prices become unaffordable.
- They don't know when new tickets are released.
- Available seats often have poor views.

Finding great tickets currently requires constant manual checking across many websites.

---

# Vision

Orchestral acts as a personal event assistant that understands your family's interests and instantly alerts you when great tickets become available.

The goal is simple:

> **Discover the right event, at the right price, with good seats, before everyone else.**

---

# Target User

Initially:

- Families
- Music fans
- Parents who are too busy to constantly monitor ticket releases

Primary notification recipient:

**Jo Wheeler (via WhatsApp)**

---

# MVP Features

### 1. Learn Your Interests

Connect Spotify.

Use listening history to identify:

- Favourite artists
- Favourite genres
- Frequently played music

Users can also manually follow artists.

---

### 2. Monitor Ticket Sources

Continuously monitor:

- New tour announcements
- Ticket releases
- Limited offers
- Additional ticket drops
- Verified resale listings

---

### 3. Personal Filters

Users configure:

- Maximum ticket price
- Maximum travel distance
- Preferred cities (e.g. Poole, Bournemouth, London)
- Number of tickets required
- Preferred seating quality

---

### 4. Smart Ticket Quality

Prioritise tickets that have:

- Good stage visibility
- Reasonable price
- Adjacent seats
- Trusted seller

Avoid recommending poor or obstructed views where possible.

---

### 5. WhatsApp Alerts

When a suitable opportunity appears, immediately send:

- Artist
- Venue
- Date
- Ticket price
- Seat information
- Why it matches your preferences
- Direct booking link

The notification should take less than 10 seconds to read.

---

# Example

**Harry Styles announces Wembley Stadium.**

Within minutes Jo receives:

> 🎵 Harry Styles - Wembley Stadium
>
> £50 per ticket
>
> Great lower-tier seats with clear stage view
>
> Saturday 18 July
>
> Tickets available now
>
> Book here →

She taps the link and buys the tickets before they sell out.

---

# Success Metrics

- Users purchase tickets they would otherwise have missed.
- Alerts are sent within minutes of availability.
- High relevance (few unnecessary notifications).
- Users trust Orkestrel enough to leave monitoring entirely to it.

# Design Principles

Orkestrel should always be:

- Fast
- Personal
- Trustworthy
- Simple
- Action-oriented

Every notification should answer four questions immediately:

1. What is it?
2. Why am I being told?
3. How much does it cost?
4. Where do I click?

If those four answers are clear, Orkestrel has done its job.
